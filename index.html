Lyric Beat Maker

One lyric line = one bar (max 16 lines). Kicks land on the stressed words.

Click cells to edit the selected bar. Click a lyric line to pick a bar.

Music

Sounds

Load your own samples (wav, mp3, m4a). They are saved on this device. The 808 plays at the pitch you set as its root note.

Settings

The key is stored only on this device and sent only to api.anthropic.com. Without a key the app uses offline rules.
